===================================================================
DECISION HELPER - RELEASE NOTES (v2.4.0)
===================================================================

[NEW FEATURES & IMPROVEMENTS]
1. Edit Area Full Modal Sheet:
   - Tap "Edit / Rename" on any category pill to open the full Edit Area sheet.
   - Customized Emoji Badges: Add emoji icons (💼, ❤️, 🏃, 💰, ✈️, 🏠, 🎯, 📚) to category pills.
   - Decision Sorting Rules: Choose custom sorting (By Score, By Date, A-Z) per category.
   - Bulk Decision Re-assignment: Move decisions into categories with simple checkboxes.

2. WhatsApp-Style Category Context Menu & Reorder Lists Modal:
   - Long-press / right-click category pills for Edit, Delete, or Reorder options.
   - Reorder Lists bottom sheet with drag handles, move up/down arrows, and green Add preset pills.

[BUG FIXES & PERMANENT STABILITY]
1. Fixed Category Resurrection Bug:
   - Deleted and renamed categories stay permanently saved across page reloads.

2. Fixed Settings Dropdown Positioning:
   - Fixed Settings gear dropdown position (`right: 16px; top: 60px; z-index: 9999`) across Home View, Workspace View, Mobile, and Desktop.

3. Fixed Decision Trash Can Button:
   - Isolated direct click handlers so clicking 🗑️ on decision cards deletes decisions without triggering card navigation.

4. Un-nested Edit Area Modal:
   - Moved Edit Area modal to top-level HTML body so it opens reliably on all browsers.
